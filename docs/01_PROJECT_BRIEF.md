# Project Brief: Social Media Data Analysis

## One-line summary
Analyze likes, comments and posts to understand what content works and why people engage.

## Problem statement
Creators and brands post constantly but do not know what drives engagement. Data can show which formats, times and topics perform best.

## Learning goals
Exploratory data analysis, engagement metrics, text and hashtag analysis, sentiment, correlation versus causation, and storytelling with charts.

## Target users
- Content creators and social media managers
- Marketing analysts

## In scope
- Load posts data (date, type, caption, likes, comments, shares, followers)
- Compute engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares) / followers
- Analyse by post type, hour of day, day of week and hashtag
- At least 5 clear charts and a written list of insights

## Out of scope (for v1)
- Production-scale infrastructure, authentication and payments
- Anything under "Could have" in `02_REQUIREMENTS.md` unless time remains

## Success criteria
- 5+ charts, each with a one-sentence takeaway
- 3+ actionable recommendations
- Findings reproducible by running one script
