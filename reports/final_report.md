## 2. Abstract

This project explores how post format, timing and text relate to social-media engagement. The reproducible workflow generated and cleaned 600 synthetic posts, calculated engagement rate, and created descriptive charts, text summaries and a lightweight regression. Video posts had the highest mean synthetic engagement rate at 8.54%, while the observed peak hour was 18:00 at 10.25% across 21 posts. On a seeded 120-post holdout, linear regression achieved MAE 0.01782 compared with 0.02318 for a training-mean baseline. Because the data is synthetic and encodes patterns by construction, the findings validate the workflow rather than establish advice for a real account.

## 3. Introduction

Creators and social-media teams need a consistent way to summarize interaction rates and compare content patterns. This project demonstrates a small, reproducible exploratory workflow and a dashboard, with explicit reminders that observational summaries do not show causation.

## 4. Data

- **Source:** synthetic data generated by `src/generate_data.py`; no third-party or personal data was used.
- **Size and period:** 600 posts from 2024-01-02 through 2024-12-29.
- **Fields:** timestamp, format, caption, hashtags, followers, likes, comments and shares.
- **Cleaning:** the generated file had no duplicate IDs or invalid required values in the inspected run; all 600 records remained. Missing hashtag text is treated as no hashtags.
- **Limitations:** one synthetic account, intentionally simulated patterns, no audience/context variables, no representativeness of a platform or real account.

## 5. Method

Engagement rate is `(likes + comments + shares) / followers`, with zero for zero followers. The workflow reports means with sample counts by type, hour, weekday, hashtag count and hashtag; measures caption length in words; and ranks observed top/bottom posts with context. A linear regression uses post type, weekday, hour, hashtag count and caption word count to predict engagement rate. It is compared with a training-mean `DummyRegressor` on an 80/20 seeded random holdout (480 training, 120 test). Preprocessing is fit within the training pipeline. No VADER sentiment score is reported because its package was unavailable in the offline build environment.

## 7. Discussion

The descriptive workflow and held-out comparison ran successfully. The regression beat the baseline on this single split, but the sample is small and synthetic, so its apparent predictive value may not transfer. The synthetic generator deliberately associates engagement with several included predictors; results therefore confirm the pipeline can recover programmed relationships. The 18:00 peak has only 21 posts. No real-world recommendations should be adopted without validation.

## 8. Limitations and ethics

Use only data the account owner is authorized to analyze. Remove usernames and personal identifiers before loading real exports. Keep engagement rate comparisons in context: follower counts, format mix, timing, paid promotion and audience composition can confound comparisons. The project stores generated data locally and excludes datasets from Git. Do not interpret association as causation.
